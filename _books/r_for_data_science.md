The definitive guide to doing data science in R. Wickham and co-authors walk you through the entire data analysis workflow — importing, tidying, transforming, visualizing, and communicating results — using the tidyverse ecosystem throughout. The consistency of the tidyverse philosophy makes everything feel cohesive: once you internalize the pipe operator and the grammar of graphics, the rest of the book clicks into place naturally.

The chapters on `dplyr` and `ggplot2` alone are worth the read. The explanations are clear without being condescending, and the examples use real datasets that make the techniques feel immediately applicable rather than academic. The section on tidy data is one of the best explanations of data structure I've come across — it reframes how you think about organizing data for analysis.

The 2nd edition adds meaningful updates including `tidyr` pivoting, `lubridate`, and better coverage of iteration with `purrr`. A must-read for anyone doing serious data work in R.
